**Provenance caveat.** All 12 arrived as Pinterest-hash filenames with no URL. Brands below are read off the image itself. Origin studio and date are **not verified** for any of them except the amfAR invite, which prints its own date. Until each is traced to a source, treat these as vibe evidence, not citable references.

| 1 | `creatsy-tape-mockup` | Creatsy stock mockup of four packing tapes | unknown | Each roll is its own colourway and pattern, yet they read as one family. | 2 | `charlemagne-bag` | Cobalt paper bag, blind-embossed wordmark | unknown | One colour does the whole exterior. The wordmark is broken across two lines ("Charle / magne") and embossed, so it is found by light, not ink. Contrast is saved for the handle and the lining (burnt orange). |
| 3 | `amfar-cannes-invite-box` | amfAR Gala Cannes 30th invitation box | 2024-05 (printed on piece; older than 12 months) | One red in four materials: flock, acrylic, foil, card. The type is bigger than the doors, so opening the box splits the word. A disc seal is the only "logo". |
| 4 | `fomo-shipper-boxes` | FOMO energy drink shipper cartons | unknown | Two colours per variant, chosen to clash (lime/red, violet/red). The liquid pattern wraps across edges as if the box were cut from a bigger sheet. Fat type, three sizes only. Lime and violet are already Following's colours. |
| 5 | `les-bons-vivants-boxes` | Les Bons Vivants cheese subscription boxes | unknown | Bone exterior with one line of cobalt handwriting. Inner lid carries a ruled table of contents. The base is a flood of cobalt or marbling. Quiet outside, loud inside. |
| 6 | `blue-yellow-cropped-bag` | Paper bag with a cropped script stroke | unknown | A fragment of a letterform scaled past the edges until it stops being a letter and becomes a pattern. Two colours. No logo lockup anywhere on the face. |
| 7 | `zedcrest-drawer-box` | Zedcrest Wealth drawer gift box | unknown | Slogan set full-bleed in black gloss on black matte. The only colour is a small gold seal. Ribbon pull makes the opening a gesture. The slogan itself is a cheap joke; the tone-on-tone scale is the lesson. |
| 8 | `lime-led-palette` | Six-colour palette, proportioned | unknown | Proportion is the point: lime owns about 37% of the field, black and white share the middle, and orange, pink and purple are small. A palette is a ratio, not a list. |
| 9 | `rhode-bag-tissue` | rhode shopping bag and printed tissue | unknown | Wordmark turned 90 degrees, run up the left edge and bled off the top. Nothing is centred. The tissue repeats the wordmark small, so the bag has a second, quieter surface. Handle is tonal. |
| 10 | `starface-pr-box` | Starface PR mailer, shot from above | unknown | The interior is a colour-blocked tray: each product gets its own coloured compartment. It is composed for the overhead phone photo, which is how it actually gets seen. |
| 11 | `sunwhip-mailer` | Sunwhip PR mailer | unknown | The inner lid is the billboard: script logo at full width, one line of copy under it. Two colours only (orange, lime). The tray is the same orange, so products look set into the box. |
| 12 | `bubble-summer-box` | Bubble "Once Upon a Summer" PR box | unknown | Illustration lives inside, as one scene across lid and tray, with flat offset shadows. The outside does not need it. Checkerboard tray gives the products a floor to sit on. |
