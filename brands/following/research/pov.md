# Following: point of view

Written 2026-10-12 from the 12 images in `Vibe Digestion`, the `Following Guidelines` cloud file, and the August packaging brief (V3).

## The idea

**The box is a set, not a container.**

Following's audience films what it receives. A PR box, a bag, a card is going to be held up to a phone and posted. So every Following object is designed for the shot first and for storage second. That is the one thing a creator platform can say with packaging that a skincare brand cannot.

## What the 12 images have in common

Eleven of the twelve are objects, photographed. None is a logo sheet. Read together they make six rules.

1. **One colour owns the surface.** Cobalt bag, red box, oxblood bag, orange mailer, yellow lid. The dominant colour takes well over half the field. The palette image says it with numbers: lime is about 37% and the three accents together are about a third of that.
2. **The second colour is a clash, and it is rationed.** Cobalt with burnt orange, orange with lime, violet with red, blue with yellow. Two colours per surface. Never three fields.
3. **The type is bigger than the thing.** Wordmarks run off the edge (rhode), break across lines (Charlemagne), split when the doors open (Cannes), or crop down to a single stroke (blue/yellow bag). A small centred logo appears in none of them.
4. **Quiet outside, loud inside.** Bone box with a cobalt flood. Yellow lid with a four-colour tray. The inner lid is treated as the billboard, the tray as the stage.
5. **Flat ink, real material.** No gradients, no shadows, no 3D renders. The richness comes from emboss, flock, gloss-on-matte, ribbon, tissue.
6. **A family of colourways, not one design.** FOMO, the tapes and Starface all work as sets where each member has its own colour pair and the structure stays fixed.

## What they reject

- Dark navy as a default "premium" ground
- Small marks scattered evenly across a panel
- Photography on packaging
- Thin or light type weights
- Illustration on the exterior (it appears once, in Bubble, and only inside)

## Where Following is today

**Working already**
- The colour pair is right. Lime `#D3FF02` and violet `#5100F3` on ink `#050911` is the same clash FOMO uses, and nobody else in UAE influencer marketing owns it.
- The wordmark is wide, lowercase and heavy enough to survive being cropped or embossed.
- The headline voice is set: ExtraBold line, then a MediumItalic line. It is the most recognisable habit in the file.
- Two shapes recur and can be named: the rotated pill tag and the big arc that rises from the bottom edge.

**Not working**
- The "Guidelines" file contains no guidelines. Logo, six colour circles and one tagline sit loose on the pasteboard; the 21 artboards are app-store screenshots.
- There are three palettes in circulation: the six circles in the file, the navy/acid-lime/orange of packaging V3, and the UI kit inside the screenshots (a second lime `#CDFF3A`, a second black `#181714`).
- There are two display typefaces in circulation: Plus Jakarta Sans in the file, Urbanist in packaging V3.
- Gradients are still in use (cream fade on the Google Play story, a gradient circle on the pasteboard, mesh gradients in the 2023 asset folder).

## The tension to exploit

Following is a money product (cashback, payouts, AED balances) dressed for people whose job is taste. Fintech defaults to navy and trust. Creator brands default to pastel and cute. The inspo sits in neither: it is saturated, flat, loud and adult. Following should look like **the loudest thing in the unboxing video and the most precise thing in the banking app**, using the same three colours for both.

## What this means for packaging V3

V3 is competent and it is the opposite of this folder. Its first rule is "small, many, everywhere" and "the artwork never leads"; its ground is navy; lime is demoted to an edge. The inspo says one large gesture, a saturated ground and lime in charge. I would not patch V3. I would keep its discipline (unit grid, one-accent-per-panel rule, the one-surface-is-busy rule) and replace its look.
