# Inflink: brief

Current as of 2026-10-31. Source of truth for the look: `C:\Users\user\Desktop\Inflink Design System\START-HERE.md` (www.inflink.ae). Older docs in the repo (`DESIGN.md` "Quiet Power", Inter + Instrument Serif, `inflink_brand_system_v2.pdf`) are superseded.

## What it is
The link in bio tool built for creators. Links for the audience, and a storefront brands can book and pay through, inside the same link. Operated by Following FZC. Powered by Following.

## Audience
- Creators in the UAE: sell Reels, Stories, Posts, UGC, venue visits, consults, merch. Pain: DMs, chasing payment.
- Models: digitals with dates, a diary, booking and cancellation terms.
- Brands: find creators, see prices, pay safely, approve the work.

## Competitors
Linktree, Stan, Beacons (link in bio and creator stores). Collabstr, Postcollabs, Yunova (booking and marketplaces). Full teardown: `NEWINFLINK/docs/website-rebuild/RESEARCH.md`.

## Must-haves
- Urbanist only. Canvas #FAFAF7, violet #6D5EF8, peach #FF8F6B, deep indigo #1C1840. Aurora, Stage, Fluted orb, Claim input, Powered by Following.
- Copy: British spelling, sentence case, numerals, AED 1,200. No em dashes, exclamation marks or emojis. No invented stats, testimonials, logos or creators. Numbers only from `facts.ts`.
- The people in renders are AI-generated and fictional: never present them as real Inflink creators.

## Must-avoid
Lists of rows, text-heavy sections, serif accents, bare-number headlines, words: unlock, elevate, seamless, empower, supercharge, game-changer, revolutionise, next-level.
