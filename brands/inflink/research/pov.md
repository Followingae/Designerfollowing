# Inflink POV

**The category now.** Link in bio tools fight on features and fees. Linktree owns the name, and its 2022 Collins identity is loud self-expression. Stan and Beacons have moved from links to creator stores. Dubai marketplaces (Postcollabs, Yunova) sell verified creators and escrow, and look like directories.

**What everyone does.** Bright SaaS colour, phone mockups, creator faces, "all in one". Soft gradient fields are now common across fintech and AI, so Aurora alone will not be remembered.

**White space.** Nobody owns the moment a creator and a brand meet and money moves safely. Linktree sends people away. Inflink closes the deal inside the link.

**The tension to use.** Soft colour, real money: warm and personal on the surface, strict about payment underneath.

**What the expansion adds.** The website system is strong but carried by Aurora, which is not ownable by itself. The logo's two dots are. They become the identity: two dots, one link. A creator, a brand, and the overlap where work gets paid. Three ownable devices follow: dot windows (photos inside the two dots), the address (`inflink.ae/name` as a graphic line), and fluted glass (money held behind ribbed glass).
