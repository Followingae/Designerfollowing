# Inflink on Instagram: what the research says (2026-10-31)

**Who we talk to.** UAE creators first, models second, brands third. A creator decides in one scroll, so one plain line and one strong picture.

**What creators want to know.** How to get brand deals, what to charge, how to get paid. Guides keep returning to the same points: brands check your profile first, a clear rate card and media kit help, and Reels usually price above feed posts and Stories below. Those are all things Inflink does inside the link, so useful content and the product point the same way.

**What Instagram rewards.** Published guides agree on about 2 Reels and 3 to 5 feed posts a week, with sends and watch time as the signals that reach new people. These come from marketing blogs, not Instagram, so treat them as a starting point.

**Where competitors are.** Linktree is adding sponsored links to creator profiles. Stan and Beacons sell creator stores. Dubai marketplaces sell verified creators and escrow. Inflink's gap: the brand books and pays inside the creator's own link.

**Limits of this research.** Web search returned guides, many selling tools, and their statistics are often unsourced, so none are quoted in posts. I have not been able to review competitors' live Instagram feeds, so that still needs a manual look.
